# skyPerformance, UX wireframe

Interactive UX wireframe for **skyPerformance**: documented conversations, improvement
plans, annual evaluations and the community walk-through, for a multi-site employer.

**Live: https://yaswanthramp.github.io/skyperformance-preview/**

Use **View as** in the header to switch between **Employee**, **Manager** and **HR**, and
between managers at different levels of the chart. Switching re-scopes every screen. Every
tab, table row, filter and button is live.

Static files only: no build, no dependencies.

## The five things it does

- **Coaching** · one place to document a conversation. Six types, from recognition to a
  policy and procedure discussion. The employee reads it and acknowledges it, and the
  acknowledgement is what makes it stand up later.
- **Improvement plans** · four levels of counseling, SMART actions, an initial meeting,
  scheduled reviews and a recorded resolution. Prior coaching attaches to the plan
  automatically. Nothing reaches the employee until every approver has signed off.
- **Annual evaluations** · one per person per year, five sections scored 1 to 4, with key
  performance indicators and developmental opportunities. Below 50% is flagged.
- **Site visits** · the full community walk-through: 172 items across 17 sections, scored
  2 / 1 / 0, with flags and photographs. Available at Regional level and up.
- **To-dos** · anything scored 0, flagged or photographed on a visit becomes an action item
  on the Executive Director's own page, with an owner and a due date.

## Every name is a label

**Settings › Roles and wording** renames the three roles, the five levels and the words the
product uses. Type in a field and the left-hand navigation changes as you type. The
product ships with senior living wording because that is where it was specified; a
manufacturer would call a community a site, coaching feedback, and a Regional Director an
Area Manager, and nothing else changes.

## What to look at

- **How the product works** on the Home dashboard: document it, they acknowledge it, it
  happens again, start a plan, approve then activate, the file survives. Every step is a
  link.
- **A site visit**, Action plan tab: the findings the walk-through produced, and the button
  that puts them on the Executive Director's to-do list.
- **An improvement plan** waiting for approval: read it as the Executive Director, approve
  it, switch to HR, approve it, switch to the department head, activate it, then switch to
  the Employee and see it appear.
- **Org chart**: drawn from the system of record, read only. People outside your reporting
  line show where they sit and nothing else.
- **Records**: one place to answer "what has been documented about this person between
  these dates".

## Sample data

Riverbend Senior Living is a fictional company. Every person, community, number and
quotation is invented. No real employee record, customer or vendor appears anywhere in it.
